Five Nightsat Komarek

You took the night job guarding Meme Arcade. After closing, the meme mascots wander the building. Watch the cameras, work the doors, and answer questions to keep the generator running until 6 AM.

How to survive

  • Power drains all night. Cameras, doors and lights drain it faster.
  • Answer questions to make power. A wrong answer costs power, makes noise, and pulls a mascot closer. You'll see that question again later.
  • Check the cameras to track the mascots. When one leaves a hallway camera, it's at your door.
  • Use the lights to see who's at a door, and close the door until they give up and leave.
  • Survive until 6 AM to unlock the next night. Each night the mascots get faster. Beat Night 5 to win. If the power runs out, it's over.

Keys: 1–4 answer · C cameras · Q/E doors · A/D lights · Esc pause
